01
Ignite — Who are you
Weeks 1–2 · No product. Build presence. Make them curious before they see the shirt.
No product shown yet. This phase is entirely about brand character. If people don't feel the brand here, nothing in Phase 3 will work.
Week 01
Tuesday Static Brand Voice
Brand opening post — the mark
Close-up of the KWOSK logotype on a dark flat lay. No explanation. Let the arrows in the K do the talking. One image, full bleed, no text overlay.
Caption: 穿得好，不需要人看出来。
Tags: 3–5 niche urban fashion tags only. No flood.
Objective: Awareness
Friday Reel Brand Voice
The man, not the brand — founder intro
30 sec. Not a face reveal. Hands working. Designing. A shirt folded. Text overlay: "I don't make clothes for everyone." No talking to camera. No pitch.
Mood: Slow, minimal music. Low key lighting.
No sell. Pure atmosphere.
Objective: Awareness
Week 02
Tuesday Static The Craft
Design process — the sketch
Flat lay of sketchbook, reference imagery, or raw design file screenshot. Black and white mood. Show that thought went into this.
Caption: Something is being made. No reveal date. Build tension.
Objective: Trust
Friday Reel The Craft
300GSM — weight of intention
30 sec. Close up: fabric held, dropped, pressed. Voiceover or text: "Most tees are forgotten. This one stays." No price. No buy link.
Cinematic grade. No product reveal yet.
Objective: Trust
02
Tension — Something is coming
Weeks 3–4 · Product teasers. No pricing. Build desire before access. Influencer seeding begins.
Desire is built here, not in Phase 3. If Phase 2 is weak, launch day underwhelms. Seed influencer shirts Week 3 so their content lands inside your Phase 3 window.
Week 03
Tuesday Static The Wearing Man
Design teaser — partial graphic reveal
Cropped shot of a graphic — 40% of design visible maximum. Dark background. High contrast. Do not show the full design. Make them want to see more.
Caption: 一件衬衫，一个选择。Not yet.
Objective: Awareness
Friday Reel Brand Voice
Brand film — Concept A or B ★
"Employee of the Month" or "Signal Lost." Your cinematic centrepiece. 45–60 sec cut for IG. This is your highest-effort, highest-reward post. Do not skip or delay it.
Caption: Minimal. Let the film do the work.
★ This post cannot be replaced by anything else. If shoot isn't ready, delay the whole calendar — don't post something weaker in its place.
Objective: Awareness
Week 04
Tuesday Static The Curation
The Curation — identity flat lay
A mood flat lay: book, coffee, keys, headphones — not the shirt. Identity of the man who wears Kwosk, without showing Kwosk. Let the audience self-identify.
Caption: Chinese. One line about knowing who you are before others do.
Objective: Awareness
Friday Reel The Craft
Full graphic reveal — first look
First full look at the graphic. Cinematic slow reveal. Pan from fabric texture to full print. No price yet. This is the moment they've been waiting for since Week 3.
Caption: 设计，不是装饰。它是态度。
Story: Add link to product page or waitlist same day.
Objective: Trust
03
Launch — It's here. Choose.
Weeks 5–6 · Product is live. Conversion is the job. Every post links to purchase.
Influencer content should be landing in this window. If it's not, you seeded too late. Every post now carries a quiet but direct CTA. Quiet does not mean weak.
Week 05 — Launch week
Tuesday Static The Wearing Man
Launch static — editorial product shot
Worn, not flat lay. Man in context — urban, still, intentional. Not a lookbook smile. The shirt is present but the man is the subject. This is your first hard-sell post.
Caption: RM108. 300GSM. 限量。Link in bio.
Make it quiet but direct. No exclamation marks.
Objective: Convert
Friday Reel The Wearing Man
First wear — sensory reel
The shirt removed from packaging. Unfolded. Put on. Feels like a ritual, not an unboxing. 20–30 sec. No voiceover. Let sound design work. Every movement is intentional.
Caption: 只剩 X 件。— creates urgency without promotional red.
Story: Direct product link added same day.
Objective: Convert
Week 06
Tuesday Static Social proof
Social proof — influencer or first buyer
Repost influencer content or a buyer's photo. Keep it raw. Do not over-polish. If no influencer content yet — packed orders, garment tag close-up, or receipts. Don't fake it.
Caption: One line maximum. Let the proof speak.
Objective: Social proof
Friday Reel The Craft
Design meaning — behind the graphic
30 sec. Explain — without explaining — what the tattoo-inspired design represents. Visual montage + text overlays. No over-explaining. This converts the last fence-sitters who need one final nudge of meaning.
End with: Link in bio. Nothing else.
Objective: Convert
+
Stories — parallel track
Not counted in the 2/week cadence. Runs alongside the feed. Keeps the account alive on non-post days.
Countdown sticker
Week 4 onward. Black background. Just the date. No copy. Repost to your story every single day until launch.
Behind-the-shoot BTS
Same day as any shoot. Raw clips, 5–15 sec each. Costs almost nothing. Keeps account visible between feed posts.
Launch day — direct link
On launch day: stories with direct Shopify link. One per design. Black background, product image, price, link. Minimal.
Repost influencer stories
When influencers post, repost immediately. First 24 hours is your window. Do not delay. Every hour matters.
What will kill this plan
  • Skipping the brand film. It's the only post doing heavy awareness work you cannot replicate with statics. If it's not ready, delay the calendar. Don't substitute.
  • Showing the price before you've built desire. Phase 1 and 2 have zero price mentions. That's intentional.
  • Missing a Tuesday or Friday and telling yourself you'll post double next week. You won't. One missed post breaks the pattern your audience is forming.
  • Seeding influencers too late. If shirts go out in Week 5, their content arrives after your launch spike. Worthless. Seed in Week 3.
  • Using red for any promotional messaging. Red is the colour of choosing on your own terms — not a sale colour. One wrong use and the brand language breaks.
